I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Delaware. My research focuses on labor economics, particularly how public policies shape employment, earnings, and gender disparities. My job market paper examines how universal pre-kindergarten affects the division of paid work and earnings within married couples. Other projects examine work authorization for international STEM graduates and salary transparency laws.

I am on the 2026–2027 economics job market.
